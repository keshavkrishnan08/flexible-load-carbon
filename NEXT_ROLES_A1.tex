\noindent\textbf{Where we are.} Week 1 delivered the spine: the
capacity-substitution channel $\KR^{*}>\KR^{\circ}$, the exact net-carbon
inequality (C), the distribution-free cutoffs, the closed-form thresholds
($\bar C_M,\CR^{\dagger},\CR^{\circ},\bar C_M^b$), a 15/15 machine-precision audit,
and a 3-referee honesty pass --- cross-validated by all four roles. An adversarial
\emph{Management Science} review puts us at $\approx$70--75\% of the rigor bar.
The gap is one keystone (a general-distribution, finite-optimum carbon theorem)
plus robustness, a clean numerical narrative, and positioning. Week 2 closes it.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Scope (hard wall).} A1 only: general theory + numerical methods.
\emph{Not} A2 (empirical calibration / real-data magnitudes), \emph{not} B1
(reliability, welfare, social-vs-private pricing), \emph{not} B2 (flexibility
continuum, AI, storage, multi-grid, dynamics). Every task below is theory or
numerical-method work on the A1 model.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Parallelization.} The four roles are designed to run
simultaneously and share only notation + the Week-1 closed forms. Role~1 (lead)
carries the keystone; Roles~2--4 consume Role~1's conditions \emph{as they land}
but can start immediately on their independent components.

%====================================================================
\role{Role 1 --- Lead General Theory \;\textnormal{\small(keystone; carries the heaviest load)}}{%
Turn the headline from ``conditional/conjectural for general $F$'' into a proved,
finite-optimum, general-distribution theorem. The paper's title depends on this role.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Existence \& uniqueness of the interior optimum.} Prove coercivity
        of the profit $\Pi$ and give a primitive condition on $F$ (e.g.\
        log-concave density, or monotone hazard) under which any interior critical
        point is the unique global maximizer --- discharging the ``interior
        optimum is assumed'' gap and supplying Condition~2 ($\mathcal H\prec0$)
        from primitives rather than eigenvalue scans.
  \item \textbf{Promote the shift-ratio condition to a primitive.} Replace the
        endogenous-window condition ``$g(x)=F_{\KR}/f$ non-increasing on
        $(\KR^{*}{-}K_M^{*},\KR^{*})$'' with a support-wide monotone-$g$ (or
        hazard-rate) condition on $F$ that \emph{implies} it, and prove the
        implication. State it as a clean primitive hypothesis.
  \item \textbf{Prove general-$F$ non-emptiness of $\{\Delta\ge0\}$ (the keystone).}
        Under the primitive shift-ratio condition plus a tail/skew condition, prove
        carbon \emph{falls somewhere} in $\CR$ for any such $F$ --- upgrading
        Theorem~3(iii) from conjecture to theorem, or sharply characterizing the
        $F$-class for which it holds.
  \item \textbf{Finite-interior-optimum carbon win.} Exhibit a primitive condition
        under which carbon strictly falls at a \emph{finite, interior} $\KR^{*}$
        (not the $\KR^{*}{=}\infty$ knife-edge corner), so the result is not an
        unbounded-capacity artifact.
  \item \textbf{State the main theorem; re-scope the abstract/title} to match
        exactly what (1)--(4) deliver, and write the economic-intuition narrative
        (why $R>c$ + negative demand--supply association $\Rightarrow$ carbon
        falls) at journal quality.
\end{itemize}

%====================================================================
\role{Role 2 --- Distributional Robustness Theory}{%
Close the cross-distribution robustness layer that Week 1 left at ``fixed
capacities.''}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Sign the capacity covariation} $dK^{*}/dF$ along the shape class
        $\mathcal U$, converting the least-favorable result from a fixed-capacity
        statement into a genuine cross-$F$ \emph{least-favorable-distribution}
        theorem (or prove the obstruction precisely).
  \item \textbf{Robust certificate.} Turn the magnitude bounds into a guarantee
        $\Delta\ge0$ valid for \emph{every} $F\in\mathcal U$ with $\CR\le\CR^{\dagger}$,
        making the closed-form condition a true distributionally-robust certificate.
  \item \textbf{Repair the $\mathbb E[\text{fossil}^{*}]\to0$ limit} with a
        dominated-convergence / finite-$\KR$ envelope argument, removing the
        a.s.-corner hand-wave in the threshold proof.
  \item \textbf{Position the robustness machinery} against the
        least-favorable-distribution / distributionally-robust-optimization
        literature (feeds Role~4's bibliography).
\end{itemize}

%====================================================================
\role{Role 3 --- Numerical Methods \& Reproducibility}{%
One coherent, reproducible numerical backbone that demonstrates the theory at a
finite interior optimum.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{One carbon-sign narrative.} Replace the scattered ``2/32, 10/40''
        with a principled sweep over Role~1's primitive condition on a curated
        distribution family, showing carbon falls at a \emph{finite interior}
        optimum --- one consistent story across Roles~1/3/4.
  \item \textbf{Hessian certificate.} A reproducible script confirming
        $\mathcal H\prec0$ on the interior path for Role~1's distribution class
        (backstops the existence/regularity proof).
  \item \textbf{Build the reversed-channel counterexample.} Construct a realized
        $F$ with $g$ increasing $\Rightarrow \KR^{*}<\KR^{\circ}$ --- the
        sharpness example Week~1 could not produce (algebraic sign-flip is not a
        realized example).
  \item \textbf{Solver hardening + one-command harness.} Replace finite-difference
        cofactors with analytic ones (kills the noisy knife-edge points), document
        tolerances, and ship a single \texttt{reproduce} command covering the 15
        exact-formula checks plus the carbon sweep.
\end{itemize}

%====================================================================
\role{Role 4 --- Consistency, Regimes \& Positioning}{%
Make the four pieces one manuscript a referee reads as a single, consistent
paper.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Unify the cross-role statements.} One Assumption~1
        ($R>c+\CM$) everywhere, one carbon decomposition (Role~1's two-force
        split), and strip any out-of-scope miner/oligopoly material --- a single
        notation table and regime map for the whole manuscript.
  \item \textbf{Complete the regime map} with the finite-optimum statement from
        Role~1, so small-FCL / interior / full-coverage and the boundedness axis
        ($\CR$) compose into one consistent theorem.
  \item \textbf{Write the literature section} (theory side: capacity investment
        \& peak-load pricing, monotone comparative statics, interruptible/
        demand-response load, distributionally-robust / least-favorable methods)
        and add the bibliography --- currently zero citations.
  \item \textbf{Assemble \& edit the manuscript:} move all ``verified to $10^{-k}$''
        out of proof bodies into the numerical section, self-contain the draft
        corrections, and align every abstract claim with the theorem it cites.
\end{itemize}

\emph{primitive shift-ratio condition} (unblocks Role~3's sweep and Role~2's

\emph{Management Science} referee cannot reject on theory/rigor once Role~1
(keystone) and Role~4 (consistency + positioning) land; Roles~2--3 turn an
acceptance into a strong one.
