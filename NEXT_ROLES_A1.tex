\noindent\textbf{Status.} Week 1 delivered the spine: the capacity-substitution
channel $\KR^{*}>\KR^{\circ}$, the exact net-carbon inequality (C), the
distribution-free cutoffs, the closed-form thresholds
($\bar C_M,\CR^{\dagger},\CR^{\circ},\bar C_M^b$), a 15/15 machine-precision audit,
a 3-referee honesty pass, and a green four-role integration. An adversarial
\emph{Management Science} review puts us at $\approx$70--75\% of the rigor bar.
The gap is one keystone --- a general-distribution, finite-optimum carbon theorem
--- plus robustness, a clean numerical narrative, and theory positioning.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Scope (hard wall --- A1 only).} Every task below is general
\emph{theory} or \emph{numerical-method} work on the FCL carbon model. \emph{Out
of scope:} real-data calibration/magnitudes (A2); reliability, welfare,
social-vs-private optimum (B1); flexibility continuum, AI, storage, multi-grid,
dynamics (B2). Roles stay in their own lane below.

%====================================================================
\role{Role 1 --- General Carbon Theory \;\textnormal{\small(lead; carries the heaviest load)}}{%
Turn the headline from ``conditional for general $F$'' into a proved,
finite-optimum, general-distribution theorem --- the paper's title depends on it.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Existence \& uniqueness.} Prove coercivity of profit $\Pi$ and a
        primitive condition on $F$ (e.g.\ log-concave density) under which any
        interior critical point is the unique global maximizer.
  \item \textbf{Primitive shift-ratio condition.} Replace the endogenous-window
        ``$g(x)=F_{\KR}/f$ non-increasing'' with a support-wide condition on $F$
        that \emph{implies} it, and prove the implication.
  \item \textbf{General-$F$ non-emptiness (keystone).} Under that primitive plus a
        tail/skew condition, prove carbon \emph{falls somewhere} in $\CR$ for any
        such $F$ --- upgrading the current conjecture to a theorem.
  \item \textbf{Finite-optimum carbon win.} Give a primitive condition under which
        carbon strictly falls at a \emph{finite, interior} $\KR^{*}$ (not the
        $\KR^{*}{=}\infty$ corner).
  \item \textbf{Main theorem + re-scope.} State the headline theorem, align the
        abstract/title to exactly what is proved, and write the economic intuition
        at journal quality.
\end{itemize}

%====================================================================
\role{Role 2 --- Solver \& Numerical Methods}{%
One coherent, reproducible numerical backbone demonstrating the theory at a
finite interior optimum.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{One carbon-sign narrative.} Replace the scattered ``2/32'' counts
        with a principled sweep over Role~1's primitive condition on a curated
        family, showing carbon falls at a finite interior optimum.
  \item \textbf{Hessian certificate.} A reproducible check that $\mathcal H\prec0$
        on the interior path for Role~1's distribution class (backstops existence).
  \item \textbf{Analytic-cofactor solver.} Replace finite-difference cofactors with
        analytic ones (removes the noisy knife-edge points); document tolerances.
  \item \textbf{One-command reproducibility.} A single \texttt{reproduce} harness
        covering the 15 exact-formula checks plus the carbon sweep.
\end{itemize}

%====================================================================
\role{Role 3 --- Comparative Statics \& Robustness}{%
Close the cross-distribution robustness layer Week 1 left at ``fixed capacities,''
and deliver the sharpness example.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Cross-$F$ least-favorable.} Sign the capacity covariation
        $dK^{*}/dF$ along the shape class $\mathcal U$, turning the fixed-capacity
        result into a genuine least-favorable-distribution theorem.
  \item \textbf{Robust certificate.} Make $\CR\le\CR^{\dagger}\Rightarrow\Delta\ge0$
        hold for \emph{every} $F\in\mathcal U$, or prove the obstruction cleanly.
  \item \textbf{Reversed-channel counterexample.} Construct a realized $F$ with
        $g$ increasing $\Rightarrow \KR^{*}<\KR^{\circ}$ --- the sharpness example
        Week~1 could not produce.
  \item \textbf{Repair the limit.} Replace the $\mathbb E[\text{fossil}^{*}]\to0$
        a.s.-corner step with a dominated-convergence / finite-$\KR$ argument.
\end{itemize}

%====================================================================
\role{Role 4 --- Theory Hardening, Regimes \& Positioning}{%
Make the four pieces one manuscript a referee reads as a single, consistent
paper.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Unify the manuscript.} One Assumption~1 ($R>c+\CM$) and one carbon
        decomposition (Role~1's two-force split) everywhere; one notation table.
  \item \textbf{Complete the regime map.} Fold Role~1's finite-optimum statement
        into the small-FCL / interior / full-coverage map plus the $\CR$
        boundedness axis, as one consistent theorem.
  \item \textbf{Theory positioning.} Write the related-work section and
        bibliography on the \emph{methods} A1 uses --- monotone comparative statics
        (Topkis/Milgrom--Shannon), interruptible/demand-response economics, and
        distributionally-robust / least-favorable-distribution analysis.
  \item \textbf{Assemble \& edit.} Move all ``verified to $10^{-k}$'' out of proof
        bodies into the numerical section; align every claim with its theorem.
\end{itemize}

%====================================================================
\hd{Sequencing}
\noindent All four start now and share only notation $+$ the Week-1 closed forms.
Role~1 delivers two primitives early --- the primitive shift-ratio condition
(unblocks Role~2's sweep and Role~3's class $\mathcal U$) and the existence

hand Role~4 their final statements for assembly. \textbf{Acceptance gate:} a
referee cannot reject on rigor once Role~1 (keystone) and Role~4 (consistency $+$
positioning) land; Roles~2--3 turn acceptance into a strong one.
